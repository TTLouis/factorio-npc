## Boundaries

Assignments, enrollment, exact endpoint compatibility, bounded delivery and
whole-cell commissioning are distinct. Receipt-backed enrollment and typed
production blocks have focused host coverage; the lifecycle native fixture
exercises its declared exact placement/cleanup path, not arbitrary factory
enrollment. The delivery fixture declares synthetic initial hardware, stock and
steam, reads recipes and mechanics live, and holds the world unmodified between
its positive frames. It cannot establish a sustained fuel chain, continuous
service or autonomous commissioning.

Generic conversion remains refused. Fluid, heat and shared electrical consumer
cleanup remain unknown where no bounded native proof exists. Direct item custody
and a supported closed electrical delivery window do not authorize those cases.
An unfueled cold supplier whose native drop target is unavailable remains
unverified; the productive-supplier recovery fixture does not solve that startup
case or infer connection from placement candidates.
Cold rollback of an entire self-consistent canonical file still requires an
external durable watermark. No provider trial, publication or deployment is
authorized or claimed by this addendum.
