## Approved invariant

New production drafts declare typed `obligations`: intended outcome predicates
and exact identities to preserve. The runtime captures native geometry before
commit and seals it to the originating goal/plan/version/semantic step. JIT and
prediction compile the same contract; shared validation rejects added or removed
predicates. A declared future global count remains a valid outcome. Legacy valid
contracts remain readable/executable; legacy unproven residual defects cannot
mint a certificate.

Preservation currently supports non-fluid furnaces and mining drills/inserters
with native item geometry. A missing lazy target remains advisory unknown and
does not block a needed first fuel action. Fresh native target resolution may
refine that unknown only to an unchanged identity already in the frozen
preservation set; the refinement is durably recorded before proceeding. A known
target cannot later disappear or change, including after restart. Residual defect
certification requires resolved native targets; unknown is never connection proof.
Identity, position, direction, force/surface and native item routes are preserved;
changing fuel/output counters is ordinary production. Belts, poles, assemblers,
fluid structures and unknown types fail closed until their relevant topology is
implemented and proved. Native geometry adds explicit entity and actor scope.

A residual certificate requires frozen typed intent, its actual immutable origin,
an original count-translation mismatch, a correlated completed placement receipt,
fresh placed identity/scope, unchanged preserved geometry, and verified declared
outcomes. The harness records a classified contract defect, not a world change.
The old plan remains immutable history. A successor preserves exact semantic
steps, guidance, future contracts, verified prefix and receipts. It recognizes
already completed placement and continues without replay.

There is one durable claim per original semantic step, including successor IDs
and restart/restage, within the existing per-goal replacement cap. Claims flush
before drafting/commit. The grant and immutable predecessor equality are checked
again at commit. Missing proof, stale ownership, busy/uncertain work, amendments,
revoked authority or exhaustion must result in a classified visible pause.
